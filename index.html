CHAPTER 06 • EPIDEMIOLOGICAL CONCLUSIONS & PREVENTIVE PROTOCOL

Actionable Clinical Takeaways from the US CDC NHANES Oral Health Atlas

01. Protect First & Second Molars Before Age 40

Posterior molars (#02–#03, #14–#15, #18–#19, #30–#31) account for over 60% of early tooth loss between ages 35 and 55 due to furcation attachment loss and secondary caries under aging restorations.

02. Interdental Cleaning Is Non-Negotiable

Toothbrushing alone leaves interproximal periodontal pockets uncleaned. Respondents flossing 4–7 days/week retain 23.4 natural teeth in their 70s versus 17.2 teeth among non-flossers (+6.2 tooth dividend).

03. Shift Utilization from Symptomatic to Preventive

Waiting for acute pain to visit a dentist results in irreversible pulpal or periodontal destruction. Proactive recall every 6–12 months preserves the 20-tooth functional masticatory threshold past age 80.

04. Expand Preventive Subsidies for Low-PIR Cohorts

Closing the 33.6 percentage-point preventive utilization gap between low-income (<1.0 PIR) and affluent (≥4.0 PIR) households is the single highest-leverage public health intervention to prevent senior edentulism.
